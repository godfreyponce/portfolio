> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking. This is a **single-task plan**: per AGENTS.md it stays **uncommitted in the working tree** of branch `issue-79` for gate 2.

**Goal:** Replace the live vertical timeline (avatars, "Details" toggles), the Education card and the full-width loss graph with preview-d's shapes: the stick-figure gap, a "Where I've worked and studied" logo strip that lights and jumps to its entry, two-column Work Experience and Education entries, and the loss graph inside the page column under its rule.

**Architecture:** Entry data moves out of `Timeline.tsx` and `Education.tsx` into `lib/experience.ts`, so the strip and the entries share one list of ids. One server component, `EntryList.tsx`, renders the entries for both sections. A new client component, `LogoStrip.tsx`, sets `data-hot` and `data-pop` attributes on the entry `<li>` it points at; the entries style themselves from those attributes with Tailwind `data-*` and `group-data-*` variants, so no state crosses components. `LossGraph.tsx` and `StickmanRunner.tsx` keep their logic and change only their wrappers.

**Tech Stack:** Next.js 16 (App Router, `next/image`, `next/font/google`), React 19, Tailwind v4 (`@theme`, `data-*` variants), TypeScript.
